Our Story

About Akoro

Akoro Digital is a marketing agency based in New York, built specifically for high-value home service companies: contractors, builders, technicians, and service providers who are great at their work but don't have time to market it.

We build customer acquisition systems that turn local demand into booked jobs. Social media, Google ads, personal brand photography, websites, and SMS automation, all under one roof. No juggling vendors, no explaining yourself twice.

We move fast, stay direct, and focus on what actually moves the needle. Good creative backed by a clear strategy is the most reliable way to grow a home service business online, and that's what we deliver.

The name Akoro reflects how we think about our work: built to last, not just built to impress.
