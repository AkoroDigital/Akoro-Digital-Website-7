Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Akoro Digital ("Akoro Digital," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you interact with our website, services, forms, and messaging programs.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business information
  • Information submitted through our website forms
  • Information provided when requesting or discussing our services

We may also automatically collect certain information when you use our website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage information.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Communicate with you about services you have requested
  • Send appointment reminders, follow-ups, and other informational messages
  • Send marketing and promotional messages when you have provided the appropriate consent
  • Maintain and improve our website and customer experience
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, Akoro Digital may send you text messages related to the purpose for which you provided consent.

Depending on the type of consent provided, messages may include customer service communications, appointment-related messages, follow-ups, marketing messages, promotional offers, or other relevant communications.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be shared with service providers solely as necessary to provide the messaging service and other services you have requested.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our business, website, customer relationship management systems, communications, and other business functions. These providers may only use information as necessary to provide services to us and are not authorized to use your information for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or property.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or alteration. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Choices

You may request information about the personal information we maintain about you or request correction or deletion where applicable by contacting us using the information below.

You may also unsubscribe from SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Third-Party Services

Our website and services may use third-party providers for hosting, analytics, customer relationship management, communications, scheduling, advertising, or other business functions. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
